- First frame ~1.2s on DX12. Timeline from `--log info,wgpu_core=debug,wgpu_hal=debug`: Slint probes adapters by creating the DX12 instance 3 times (~400ms); our 6 pipelines compile with FXC in ~540ms, of which `haze` alone takes ~410ms (grid 43ms, others ~80ms total); femtovg's own shader ~66ms.
- wgpu `PipelineCache` is a no-op on DX12 (Vulkan only); the driver cache (D3DSCache) only covers DXBC→ISA, not the slow FXC HLSL→DXBC step. Offline DXBC/DXIL via passthrough shaders needs experimental device features — rejected as too costly.
- FXC vs DXC: DXC compiles much faster but needs dxcompiler.dll (~20MB, more process memory); keep FXC.
